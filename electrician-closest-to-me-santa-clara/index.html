Questions to ask before approving a project

A nearby search result alone doesn’t tell you what a contractor will include in the job. Use a short set of questions to make sure you understand the plan:

  • What parts of the electrical system or property will be assessed?
  • Which requested changes are included in the proposed work?
  • Is the project a repair, an installation, or part of a wider upgrade?
  • What written pricing can I review before work starts?
  • Should I ask about ongoing maintenance for this system?

One common mistake is to focus on the new feature without discussing the existing electrical setup. For a panel upgrade, rewiring project, generator, or EV charging installation, describe how you plan to use the property and ask what needs to be evaluated first. Don’t attempt to inspect wiring yourself.
