Make a quick checklist before the visit

A little preparation can make the conversation more useful. Gather details you already know, but leave testing and repairs to a professional. Helpful things to share include:

  • The location of the electrical issue or planned installation
  • When you first noticed the problem, if it is a repair
  • Any changes you’re planning, such as new lighting or an EV charger
  • Whether the work is for a home or a commercial property
  • Questions about maintenance or future electrical needs

Avoid guessing at the cause or opening electrical equipment to investigate. For a larger job, such as whole-home rewiring or a panel upgrade, describe the changes you want and ask how the property’s current system will be reviewed.
